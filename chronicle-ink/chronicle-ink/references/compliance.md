# Compliance

This page describes what the Skill does and does not do. It is not legal advice; the person who
publishes a film is responsible for it.

## AIGC labels

The Chinese Measures for Labelling AI-Generated Synthetic Content (in force since 1 September 2025) ask for
an explicit label people can see and an implicit label in the file.

- Explicit label: an "AI-generated" badge in the first 3 seconds (top left, never overlapping captions or
  cards) and an end card of the last 2 bars (about 5.5 s) whose text is the style pack's
  `ident.aigc_label` (pictures and music drawn and synthesized by code an AI agent wrote).
- Implicit label: an `AIGC` metadata item in the MP4 (`-movflags +faststart+use_metadata_tags`) whose JSON
  fields come from `src/engine/data/aigc-metadata.json`, plus a `comment` tag. `check` reads it back with
  ffprobe (`aigc_metadata_missing` under `--strict`).
- Standard status: the field set follows the national standard GB 45438-2025 (Cybersecurity technology,
  labeling method for content generated by artificial intelligence) as remembered: `Label`,
  `ContentProducer`, `ProduceID`, `ReservedCode1`, `ContentPropagator`, `PropagateID`, `ReservedCode2`, with
  `Label` value `1`. It is **not verified** (`verified: false`, no clause number). On 21 October 2026 the
  national standards public service platform (openstd.samr.gov.cn) listed the standard as in force
  (published 2025-02-28, effective 2025-09-01) but offered the full text only through an interactive page
  viewer, so no text was obtained. Until someone checks the field names, values, lengths and clause
  numbers against the official text and sets `verified: true` with the clause, every report says
  "AIGC field per GB 45438 (field set not verified)". The Skill does not claim compliance with the
  standard.

## Real people

- Deceased people keep personality rights (Civil Code of the PRC, Article 994): the Skill never invents
  quotations (a quote must be a verified claim with a tier-1 excerpt), has no voice at all (captions
  only), and draws in an illustrated ink style that cannot pass for a
  historical photograph.
- Living people: no portrait of an identifiable living person, ever (hard floor). A person entity without
  a death date whose birth is within 150 years, or unknown, is treated as living; shots whose scene declares
  `params.depicts_person` are refused by the script gate (`portrait_forbidden`, exit 9), and the visual
  self-review always checks "no identifiable real living person". People are drawn only as silhouettes
  without facial features.

## Maps

No national borders or administrative boundary lines in any picture (hard floor). Map-like shots show
terrain, rivers and city or site points only; every visual check has a "no borders" item. Disputed
regions are never drawn as territories.

## Buildings, light shows and brands

- Freedom of panorama differs by country, and some modern buildings and light displays are protected (the
  Eiffel Tower's night illumination is a known example). This is a hint for the Agent's judgement, not a
  rule the engine can check; landmarks with an inception of 1950 or later get the non-blocking warning
  `modern_landmark_copyright_note`.
- Pictures must not show trademarks or logos (a visual check item).

## Models and music

No model is called. Pictures are drawn by scene code with the bundled kit, the music is the bundled
code-synthesized theme and the SFX are procedural; fonts are SIL OFL. Facts and excerpts keep the licences
of their sources (Wikipedia excerpts CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikidata CC0).

## Scope of topics

There is no topic allowlist or blocklist. The floors above (no living-person portraits, no borders,
verified facts only, AIGC labels) apply to every topic.
