# Lessons
Keep footpaths out by default: in a dense centre they double the file and turn every block grey. Leave tunnels and subways out, or a line runs straight through the river. Join multipolygon member ways end to end before filling, and fill with the even-odd rule, or the river loses its islands and parks fill their courtyards. Fill the sea from the coastline side rather than guessing: coastlines are open ways that only make sense with their direction. Set line widths in poster units, not metres or CSS pixels, or the print export and the 345 px embed disagree; let them grow only with the square root of the zoom. Shrink the title to the column for long names and correct centred letter-spaced text for the trailing gap. Fit the credits line to the poster width, or it overflows on a small embed. Hold each theme for a few seconds in the film and keep the camera still: line art in motion compresses badly and the card video has a size limit. Check the marks fall inside the radius before baking; the bake drops points outside the square.
Do not invent heights for the skyline: unmapped buildings stay out, and the poster says the heights are stretched; a uniform made-up base would look real and be wrong. Query building bounds, not geometry, for the skyline: a dense centre's full geometry is hundreds of megabytes and times out on the public servers, while bounds and tags in 1.2 km tiles finish and resume from the cache. Skip outlines that hold building parts, or a cathedral outline at roof height hides its towers. Count food places by group and print the totals, so a sparse city reads as sparse data rather than an empty city. Match landmark names exactly and report the ones not found; prefer the building over a same-named site or square.
