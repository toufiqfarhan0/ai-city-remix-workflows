# Prompt slots
Required string slots are city and center (coordinates, or a place an Agent can turn into coordinates). Optional slots are radius (how much of the city the poster covers, 500 to 6000 m), marks (up to five places), kinds (which of the four posters, and the skyline side), landmarks (up to four mapped building names), themes (palettes and fonts), type (the title block), film (length, reveal and theme sequence), interaction and extraPrompt. Each is bounded to 2000 characters. The bilingual templates share the same placeholders. A second example, bay-lines.json, is San Francisco with the sea filled from the coastline on the same code.
