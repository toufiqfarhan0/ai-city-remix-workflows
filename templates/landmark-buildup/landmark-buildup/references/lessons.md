# Lessons
Overlap a corner by one block depth, not the wall thickness, or every other course juts out like a saw. A run of wall that meets another part (a bastion against a tower) must be an open run, or it builds a duplicate wall inside the other part. Put a backing core behind each course so joints read as joints, not daylight; schedule it with its course so the core never appears before the stones. Give string courses and loggias a `window` inside the shaft stage, or they float before the walls reach them. Collapse unstarted pieces in the vertex shader instead of fading them; the depth material must use the same displacement or stones cast shadows before they land. Keep floodlights modest (physically based intensities): too much light flattens the coursing. Instanced meshes move vertically while falling, so switch off frustum culling for them. Do not explode a statue's thin shell in every direction: the plates turn into confetti and the silhouette is lost. Cut one side away instead and keep the far side, the limbs and the attributes in place; make the frame thick and light enough to read.
