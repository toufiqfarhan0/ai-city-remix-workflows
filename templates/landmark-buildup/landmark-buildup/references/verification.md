# Verification
Node tests check the kit on the sample, the Statue of Liberty and the campanile recipes: deterministic pieces, stage windows respected, courses rising bottom to top, openings left empty with voussoirs above them, open runs without a closing edge, corner laps of one block depth, valid recipes rejected with `data`, and the film (build window, light keys, shots and presets).
The shoot script serves the single file under the sandbox policy and drives every control with real mouse, wheel, keyboard or touch input, each with before and after screenshots, a state change and a pixel difference: the build slider, a stage jump, a view preset, orbit, zoom, the light slider (labels included), the dismantle slider and play, plus Space to pause. It covers desktop 1600 by 900 on both tiers, a 390 by 844 phone with a touch orbit and the 345 by 345 embed with taps in both languages; checks that controls are visible and hittable with no scrollbars; and records audio before and after a gesture, console errors, policy violations, external requests, deterministic capture after other states and a backward seek, the build order of the film, reduced motion, piece and draw-call budgets and frame rates. Headless timing is indicative.
