# Lessons
Smooth the mapped centre line before using it: raw OSM ways zig-zag by a metre or two and lane lines then wobble. Reject footprints whose span along the road is larger than the footprint itself, or a building by a loop of the route becomes a kilometre-long wall. Some Overpass replicas answer with no elements; treat an answer without buildings as a failure and retry elsewhere. Landmarks stand hundreds of metres off the road: draw them larger than life (`landmarkScale`) and turn the view toward them while passing, or they are hidden by trees and the street wall. Anchor ground patterns to the world (depth plus position), not to the screen, or paving swims. Keep big patterns subtle; dark waves on the sidewalk read as shadows. Prefer the landmark ahead in the HUD card over one just passed. Pick a film start where something happens in the first 15 seconds: the card cover is the first 30 seconds.
