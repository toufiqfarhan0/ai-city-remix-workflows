# Verification
Node tests check the bake geometry (projection, resampling, side of the road, footprint rejection, landmark kinds), the world built from the sample route (wall pieces, signs, smoothed frame, the glance toward landmarks), the film timeline (monotonic position, ends and hold, light arc) and the pixel font.
The shoot script serves the single file under the sandbox policy and drives every control with real mouse, wheel, keyboard or touch input, each with before and after screenshots, a state change and a pixel difference: route scrubbing, wheel speed, the light slider (labels included), next landmark, the map key and play, plus Space to pause. It covers desktop 1600 by 900 on both tiers, a 390 by 844 phone with a touch drag and the 345 by 345 embed with taps in both languages; checks that controls are visible and hittable, there are no scrollbars and the canvas is scaled by a whole number; and records audio before and after a gesture, console errors, policy violations, external requests, deterministic capture after other states and a backward seek, film progress and light, reduced motion and frame rates. Headless timing is indicative.
