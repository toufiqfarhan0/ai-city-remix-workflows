# Isometric pixel city scene

Make an isometric pixel-art city of {{city}} from this real area: {{area}}. Bake the area with `scripts/bake-area.mjs` (OpenStreetMap through Overpass: building footprints and parts with heights, roads, railways, water, coastline, parks and landmarks) into `src/city/area.json`; never invent street geometry. Landmarks to pin and describe: {{landmarks}}. Ground is a grid traced pixel by pixel; every building is a prism on its mapped footprint at its mapped height (or a height from its type), drawn far to near with the walls facing the viewer and then the roof. Windows, floor bands, roofs, trees, cars, water and the sky are drawn by code into a low-resolution buffer scaled up by a whole number with nearest-neighbour sampling. Look: {{look}}. Light: {{light}}. Film: {{film}}. Interactions: {{interaction}}.

Keep every frame a pure function of the camera (centre, zoom, turn, lift), time of day and clock, so the film (`script(t)`) seeks deterministically and the live page shares the same renderer. Label landmarks with their baked names above their roofs and hide labels that would overlap. Show the credits line "Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL)" at all times. Provide a full layout for desktop and the film, a compact layout for phones and a 345 by 345 embed, English and Simplified Chinese UI, reduced motion (no autoplay), a silent page and one offline HTML with no runtime network, storage or model calls. Say that colours and textures are drawn by code, not photographs, and keep landmark notes factual. {{extraPrompt}}
