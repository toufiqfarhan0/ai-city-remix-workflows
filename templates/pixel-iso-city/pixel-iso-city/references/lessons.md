# Lessons
Drop building outlines that hold building parts: the outline usually has no height, gets the default for its type and hides the tower's base, and the landmark then reads a 26 m height for a 367 m tower. Match pins exactly before matching substrings ("HSBC" also names a pumping station), and let each pin claim one place. Several elements share a landmark's name (outline, address point, parts): keep the tallest. Trace the ground per pixel rather than drawing grid cells as diamonds, or roads break into steps when zoomed out. Cull prisms by their base and their full height: a tower whose base is below the screen still reaches into it. Keep lit windows sparse and in runs, or night turns into noise; keep water to thin ripple lines, or close-ups fill with blobs. Fade the box edge into the sky instead of cutting it. Turn the view slowly and smooth wall lighting by facing angle, or walls flicker between lit and shade. Hide labels that would overlap, focused landmark first. Check the side of the road traffic keeps to (`driveSide`).
