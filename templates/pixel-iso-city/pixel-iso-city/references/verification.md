# Verification
Node tests check the bake helpers (projection, building types and heights, polygon and line fill, grid encoding, outline dropping, pins and landmark heights), the world built from the sample area (prisms and normals, lanes and the side of the road, trees, landmarks), the projection and its inverse for whole and partial turns, the film timeline (landmark order, holds, turn and light arc, determinism) and a rendered frame.
The shoot script serves the single file under the sandbox policy and drives every control with real mouse, wheel, keyboard or touch input, each with before and after screenshots, a state change and a pixel difference: next landmark, labels, drag to pan, wheel zoom, the turn key, the light slider (labels included) and play, plus Space to pause. It covers desktop 1600 by 900 on both tiers, a 390 by 844 phone with a touch drag and the 345 by 345 embed with taps in both languages; checks that controls are visible and hittable, there are no scrollbars and the canvas is scaled by a whole number; and records audio before and after a gesture, console errors, policy violations, external requests, deterministic capture after other states and a backward seek, film progress, reduced motion and frame rates. Headless timing is indicative.
