# Details worth preserving

- Keep the Colosseum's northern core and inner arcades anchored while individual southern bays deteriorate in their own windows. A uniform scale or fade of the whole monument loses the point.
- Roof loss, wall loss, reuse, rebuilding and planting need separate, staggered intervals per entity. A building that vanishes at a milestone reads as a scene swap.
- Reverse scrubbing must land on the same state as forward playback; derive state from the year, never from accumulated frames.
- The narration card is the explanation of what changed. Keep it readable at 1080p (about 320 px wide; 16 px description, 14 px list, 15 px landmark note) and in the real DOM of the film.
- Do not add "Explore the city" shortcut rows; they crowd the card.
- Film time drives both the year and the camera. Hold the final pullback exactly for the last two seconds so the ending does not drift.
- The recorded film is silent. A scored copy needs listening review; peak and loudness numbers do not establish musical fit.
