## 📖 Creative Direction & Storytelling

### The Brief
- **City:** Berlin (Pariser Platz / Brandenburg Gate)
- **Angle:** *Birth, growth, scars, today & Its future*
- **Story Arc:** 
  1. Sandstone foundations and twelve Doric fluted columns assemble at Pariser Platz.
  2. In 1791, Carl Gotthard Langhans completes Berlin's monumental neoclassical gateway.
  3. Johann Gottfried Schadow's copper Quadriga is hoisted, Victoria guiding four horses towards Unter den Linden.
  4. War leaves devastating bullet scars, followed by Cold War isolation in the border zone.
  5. On November 9, 1989, the Berlin Wall falls, and the Gate becomes a universal symbol of freedom.
  6. Camera returns to Pariser Platz at dusk as golden architectural floodlights ignite across the sandstone colonnade.
